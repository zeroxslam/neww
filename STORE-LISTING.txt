CheckLink tells you if a link, file or message is safe – before you open it.

• Reads the result aloud and explains why in simple words
• Smart voice assistant, password check, phishing quiz
• 8 languages: Arabic, English, German, Italian, Spanish, French, Chinese, Hindi
Your messages stay on your device.
